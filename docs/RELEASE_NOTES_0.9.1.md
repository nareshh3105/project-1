# CodeBuilders 0.9.1

Everything in 0.9.0, plus:

- **Choose where recordings are saved**: Settings, Output, "Save Recordings To". Browse for a folder, or "Use default" for the Videos folder. Saved replays go there too, and "Open Recording Folder" opens it. A folder that cannot be written to is refused with a message.

Install, fixes and known limitations are as in 0.9.0 (`docs/RELEASE_NOTES_0.9.0.md`). Screenshots still go to the Pictures folder.
